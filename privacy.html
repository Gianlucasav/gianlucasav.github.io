Privacy Policy – GB Video Publisher

Last updated: September 24, 2026

GB Video Publisher ("the App") is a private tool used exclusively by its author, Gianluca Boris, to publish his own videos to his own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts through a private Telegram bot. The App is not offered to the public.

1. Use of YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to the author's own YouTube channel (YouTube Data API v3, method videos.insert, OAuth scope youtube.upload).

  • By using the App, users agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service.
  • The App's use of information received from YouTube API Services is subject to the Google Privacy Policy (http://www.google.com/policies/privacy).

2. Information we access, collect and store

  • Videos, titles and descriptions sent by the author to the Telegram bot. They are stored only temporarily on the author's computer during publishing and are deleted immediately afterwards.
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens for the author's YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts, stored only locally on the author's computer, to upload videos on the author's behalf.
  • The App does not read, collect or store any other YouTube data (no channel data, statistics, comments, subscribers or other users' data). The only data returned by YouTube, the ID of the uploaded video, is shown once to the author as a link and is not stored.

3. How information is used and shared

Information is used only to publish the author's videos to the platforms the author selects. No information is sold, shared with or disclosed to third parties, other than sending the video to the selected platforms (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram) through their official APIs.

The App does not use cookies and does not place, access or recognize cookies or similar technologies on users' devices.

4. Revoking access and deleting data

  • Access to YouTube/Google data can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.
  • Access to TikTok and Instagram can be revoked from the security/app settings of those platforms.
  • Stored tokens can be deleted at any time by removing them from the author's computer; upon request, all stored data is deleted within 7 days. Temporary video files are deleted automatically after each publication.

5. Contact

For any question about this policy or to request data deletion: gboris1109@gmail.com
